    Add a Riot account

    Three quick steps, about a minute.

    1. 1
      Sign in at Riot

      Opens in a new tab with a fresh login screen.

      Preparing sign-in...
    2. 2
      Copy the address

      After signing in you land on a page that cannot load. That is expected. Copy the full URL from that tab's address bar.

    3. 3
      Paste it here

      It starts with http://localhost/redirect.

    Your password never reaches valorant shop. Only an encrypted sign-in token is stored so your shops can load, and you can remove the account any time.

    Import shared account

    Add a friend's account with their share code.

    • Read-only: you see their shops, never their Riot login.
    • It stays in your list until you or the owner removes it.
    • On Discord, use /share import instead.

    Delete all my data

    Permanent. This cannot be undone.

    This permanently deletes everything valorant shop stores about you:

    • Every linked Riot account and its stored sign-in tokens.
    • Your alert settings, wishlist and account order.
    • Every share code you created. Everyone you shared an account with loses access to it immediately, and the codes stop working.
    • Shared accounts you added from other people.
    • Your website sessions. You will be signed out.

    Nothing changes in VALORANT or at Riot. You can link your accounts again later, but your wishlist, alerts and shares will be gone.
